# Roadmap: Developer's Hell

## Overview

Developer's Hell moves through six runnable MVP boundaries: first prove the exact Java 25/Fabric 26.2 toolchain and offline artifact, then establish persistent encounter semantics with the complete Lecture Boss vertical slice, complete the Contract-to-Diploma campaign, add the bounded developer-tool loops, isolate and prove safe Metadata Roulette, and finish with accessibility, original assets, offline-content review, and release evidence. Each phase leaves a playable increment while protecting the four-boss campaign and one working loop for every module.

- [ ] **Phase 6: Accessible Offline Release and Showcase** - Ship the readable, public-safe, provenance-tracked production release with documentation and end-to-end evidence.

### Phase 3: Contract-to-Diploma Boss Campaign

**Goal:** Players can finish the visible four-encounter campaign, earn one durable Diploma, and replay bosses without reopening progression exploits.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on:** Phase 2
**Requirements:** CAMP-03, CAMP-04, CAMP-05, CAMP-06, JURY-01, JURY-02, CHAIR-01, CHAIR-02, CODEX-01, CODEX-02, CODEX-03, ACC-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The player follows one visible Lecture-to-Jury-to-Chairman-to-Codex sequence using current-objective text and recoverable chapter artifacts; each first completion records its checkpoint and grants its practical reward exactly once across save/reload, including recovery of a lost artifact.
  2. The player can resolve Citation Needed evidence, Scope Creep boundaries, and chained “But Why?” counters to clear the Hostile Jury, after which Signed Defense Minutes and the Evidence Binder are awarded and encounter-owned helpers, shields, and hazards are gone.
  3. The player can break Rubric Shield nodes, survive bounded Minor Revisions, and finish on Major Revisions acceptance pads to earn the Approved Revision Stamp and reusable Red Pen without inventory deletion, earlier-chapter reset, or irreversible arena damage.
  4. Inserting the Stamp into the terminal starts a skippable local sponsor celebration, visibly drains the fictional Rich ChatGPT token meter, transforms it into Codex Overdraft, and leads through tool-queue, labelled-agent, and Context Overflow/MAX Reasoning acts to one Definitely Legitimate Diploma plus permanent local Showcase and Boss Replay access; replay rewards do not become progression gates.
  5. Across all four encounters, every harmful pattern communicates its name, affected area or icon, distinct sound and caption, wind-up, and recovery without color-only meaning; death, escape, timeout, abort, or reload removes encounter-owned entities, helpers, hazards, and boss bars before a safe retry.

**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
